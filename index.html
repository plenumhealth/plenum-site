About Plenum Health

Real-world data, put to work for patients, sites, and sponsors.

Plenum Health connects real-world clinical data to the studies that need participants. Sponsors see where eligible patients are before they commit to site activation. Sites and community clinics show what their patients can bring to a study. And patients are considered for opportunities they might never have heard about.

Mission

Designing access to clinical research around the people it is meant to serve.

Vision

A future where every eligible patient is considered for the right opportunity, wherever they live or receive care.

Core principles

  1. 01

    Every patient deserves to be seen.

    Eligibility should be considered across whole populations, not only the patients who already reach a research center.

  2. 02

    Research belongs in every community.

    Community clinics and private practices belong in the network alongside academic research hubs.

  3. 03

    Data should serve people.

    Real-world data matters when it leads to a real person getting a real opportunity.

  4. 04

    Kindness is a standard.

    We work with patients, sites, and sponsors the way we would want to be treated: clearly, respectfully, and without wasted effort.
