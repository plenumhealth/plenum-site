About Plenum Health

Real-world data, put to work for patients, sites, and sponsors.

Plenum Health empowers sponsors and sites to take an active role in shaping their path to enrollment. Through ReaLayer by Plenum Health, we connect protocol requirements with real-world data across research sites, hospitals, private and group practices, helping you understand where potentially eligible patients receive care and turn that knowledge into action.

For sponsors, that means building recruitment strategies around patient availability, whether selecting new sites or expanding recruitment through existing ones. For sites, it means demonstrating your patient population’s potential for specific studies and taking the initiative to bring relevant research opportunities to your community.

Mission

Designing access to clinical research around the people it is meant to serve.

Vision

A future where every eligible patient can be considered for a clinical trial, regardless of where they live or receive care.
